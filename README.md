## 💼 Featured Projects

### 🌱 [MittiCard – AI Soil Health Advisory App](https://github.com/Rajkamal08/MittiCard)
Farmers receive soil health cards from the government — but most can't read or act on them. MittiCard changes that. Point your camera at the card, and the app instantly reads it using Groq Vision OCR, then tells you exactly which crops to grow, what fertilizers to use, and how much it'll cost — in Hindi or English. Built with a Node.js/Express + PostgreSQL backend, OTP auth, and Firebase push notifications.
> `React Native` `Node.js` `PostgreSQL` `Groq Vision API`

[![GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-Repo-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/Rajkamal08/MittiCard) [![Live](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live-Dashboard-00C9A7?style=flat-square&logo=vercel)](https://mitticard-backend.onrender.com/fpo-dashboard)

### 🫀 [PPG Signal Quality Assessment](https://github.com/Rajkamal08/PPG-Signal-Quality-Assessment)
Turns your smartphone camera into a clinical-grade pulse sensor. Uses the phone's flash and custom Kotlin bridges to capture raw optical (PPG) signals, then cleans and grades them using a Python ML backend with Butterworth filtering and Random Forests. The result: a full signal quality score without any medical hardware.
> `React Native` `Kotlin` `FastAPI` `Scikit-Learn` `SciPy`

[![GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-Repo-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/Rajkamal08/PPG-Signal-Quality-Assessment)

---

### 📄 [AI Contract Intelligence](https://github.com/Rajkamal08/rag-document-intelligence)
Legal documents are long, dense, and expensive to review. This platform lets you drop in a contract and ask it questions — using RAG with ChromaDB and SentenceTransformers to find the right clauses, and Llama-3.3 to explain the risk in plain language. Built on a React + FastAPI stack.
> `React` `FastAPI` `ChromaDB` `Llama-3.3` `Groq`

[![GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-Repo-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/Rajkamal08/rag-document-intelligence)

---

### ❤️ [Heart Failure Prediction](https://github.com/Rajkamal08/heart-failure-prediction)
An XGBoost model trained on 11 clinical biomarkers that doesn't just predict heart failure — it explains *why* using SHAP values, and generates a PDF health report for each patient. Deployed as an interactive Streamlit dashboard.
> `Python` `XGBoost` `SHAP` `Streamlit` `Scikit-Learn`

[![GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-Repo-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/Rajkamal08/heart-failure-prediction)
